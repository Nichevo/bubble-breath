# Bubble Breath

An original, modern take on the classic bubble-blowing dragon arcade game. Play solo or two-player co-op on one keyboard, or on a phone with touch buttons.

**Play:** https://nichevo.github.io/bubble-breath/

- Player 1: A / D move, W jump, Space blow
- Player 2: ← / → move, ↑ jump, Enter blow
- On phones: on-screen Move, Jump and Blow buttons; tap the screen to start

Trap enemies in bubbles, then pop them to turn them into fruit. Pop several at once for bigger points.
